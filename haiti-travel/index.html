About Haiti

The Caribbean nation with a rich history, vibrant culture and resilient spirit.

The Heart of the Caribbean

Haiti, known as the "Pearl of the Antilles," is a country of striking contrasts where mountains meet turquoise water, and a thriving art scene sits alongside historic landmarks.

As the first independent nation in Latin America and the Caribbean, and the first Black-led republic, Haiti's history is a story of resilience and freedom.

Why Visit Haiti?

  • Historic sites and monuments
  • Vibrant arts and music scene
  • Beaches and natural wonders
  • Delicious Creole cuisine
  • Warm and welcoming people

Quick Facts

  • Capital: Port-au-Prince
  • Languages: Haitian Creole, French
  • Currency: Haitian Gourde (HTG)
  • Climate: Tropical
  • Highest point: Pic la Selle (2,680 m)
